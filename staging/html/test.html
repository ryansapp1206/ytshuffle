ytShuffle

Generate a truly randomized playback order.

Why ytShuffle?

  • Youtube's bias: YouTube's algorithm loops your most-played tracks. We pull the raw video IDs to guarantee a truly randomized queue.
  • Lazy Loading: YouTube only shuffles the first chunk of a massive playlist. We fetch every single track before shuffling.
  • Complete Privacy: No need to make your private playlists public or link them to third-party services.
