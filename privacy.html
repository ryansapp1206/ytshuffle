Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Data We Access

ytShuffle requests the youtube.readonly scope via Google OAuth. We exclusively use this to fetch your YouTube playlists and their contained video IDs to generate a randomized playback queue.

2. How Your Data is Used and Stored

All data processing occurs locally within your browser. ytShuffle does not have a backend server or database. We do not store, log, or save your YouTube data. Your Google OAuth access token is stored temporarily in your browser's local storage and is automatically deleted upon signing out or when the session expires.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties. Your playlist data never leaves your personal device.

4. Third-Party Policies

By using ytShuffle, you are also bound by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke ytShuffle's access to your data at any time via your Google Security Settings.

5. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, please reach out via our Feedback & Support page.
