Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

ytshuffle ("we," "us," or "our") operates the YouTube Playlist Shuffler. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Our application uses YouTube API Services to access your YouTube library. By using ytshuffle, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We only request the youtube.readonly scope. This allows us to view your playlists and the videos within them to perform a randomization shuffle. We do not store, collect, or share any of your personal data, playlist information, or Google account details on any server. All processing happens locally in your web browser.

3. Data Retention

Since no data is stored on our end, your information is "forgotten" the moment you close your browser tab or revoke access.

4. Contact

ytshuffle is an independent project. For questions regarding this policy or data usage, please contact the developer.
